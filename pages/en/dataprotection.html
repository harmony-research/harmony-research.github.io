Cookie policy

In this policy, the term “cookies” refers to cookies and similar technologies covered by the EU directive on the processing of personal data and the protection of privacy in electronic communications.

What is a cookie?

Cookies are small text files created by the website you visit that contain data. They are stored on the visitor’s computer to provide access to various functions. A session cookie is stored temporarily while the visitor navigates the website. It is deleted when the user closes their internet browser or after a certain period of time (when the session expires). A persistent cookie remains on the visitor’s computer until it is deleted. For more information about cookies, visit www.cookiesandyou.com.

How can I reject and delete cookies?

To review or change your cookie settings for this website, click here.

As a rule, we do not use cookies to collect personal information about a visitor. If you wish, you can reject or block cookies by changing your browser settings; consult your browser’s help function for details. Please note that most browsers accept cookies automatically. To prevent cookies from being used, you may therefore need to actively delete or block them. For information about cookies in mobile browsers and how to reject or delete them, consult your mobile phone’s manual. If you reject cookies, you can continue to visit our websites, but some functions may not work as intended.
